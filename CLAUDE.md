# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude when working in this repository.

## Project overview
Preschool-Unfiltered makes learning resources for **kindergarten to grade 4** (roughly ages 5–10).
Products are sold on Teachers Pay Teachers: https://www.teacherspayteachers.com

Two audiences:
- **Kids** – the end users of every activity and game.
- **Teachers / parents** – the buyers, who need clear instructions and classroom-ready materials.

## Content rules
- Age-appropriate language and themes; short sentences, simple vocabulary.
- Large, readable fonts (e.g. a clear sans-serif or a school/primary font), high contrast.
- Instructions should be simple enough for a teacher to follow at a glance, or a child to follow with help.
- Original work only: no copyrighted characters, art, music or brands. Use only assets we created or are licensed to use.

## Printable PDFs
- US Letter (8.5" × 11"), with safe print margins (≥ 0.5").
- Must print well in black and white; color is a bonus, not a requirement.
- Include a cover/preview page and a short teacher instructions page, suited to the Teachers Pay Teachers listing.

## Web games & interactive activities
- Prefer self-contained HTML/CSS/JS (no build step) unless a project needs more.
- Must work on tablets and touch screens as well as desktop.
- **Child privacy:** no tracking, analytics, ads, accounts or external data collection.
- Basic accessibility: large tap targets, keyboard support where practical, sound optional with a mute toggle.
